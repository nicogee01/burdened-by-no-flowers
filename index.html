        Bye-week watch

        Each team's "bye hit" is how many points its best possible lineup loses that week, using every player's points per game (games played only), after bench players fill in. Kickers and defenses are left out since they get streamed. It counts depth, so a bye you can cover barely registers. The playoff odds above already include these hits.

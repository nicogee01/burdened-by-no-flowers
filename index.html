      Road to the bye

      Six teams make the playoffs. The two division winners get seeds 1–2 and a first-round bye; everyone else fights over four wild-card spots by record, with points for as the tiebreaker. The model below plays out the rest of the season 10,000 times, using each team's scoring so far, pulled toward the league average, then lowered in any week a team loses players to NFL byes (see Bye-week watch).

        Bye-week watch

        Each team's "bye hit" is how many points its best possible lineup loses that week, using every player's points per game (games played only), after bench players fill in. Kickers and defenses are left out since they get streamed. It counts depth, so a bye you can cover barely registers. The playoff odds above already include these hits.

        06 · Picks for keepers

        Keeper trades

        Before the draft you can trade picks for someone else's keeper. The buyer pays twice: the picks they send, and the round they spend keeping him. The fair price is the pick whose expected value matches the keeper's surplus.

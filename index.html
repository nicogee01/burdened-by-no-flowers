        Each team's "bye hit" is how many points its best possible lineup loses that week, using every player's points per game (games played only), after bench players fill in.         Replacement level and stability

        The two numbers that decide how much a position is worth to keep

        Replacement level is the points per game you can get for free on waivers at that position. Lower means good players are scarcer, so each one is worth more.

        Stability r measures how well a player's value one season predicts the next, from 0 (no connection, pure luck) to 1 (exactly the same). 0.5 means about half of a player's edge carries over. Keepers are a bet on next year, so higher stability means a safer keep.
